道乃久 · 中医古籍科学知识发现智能体平台

道乃久（TaoChronos）是医哲未来人工智能研究院（IMPF-AI）研发的中医古籍科学知识发现智能体平台。 它面向中医古籍的可溯源研究：先从古籍中检得原文，再据原文作答，每一条引文都注明出处，并与原文逐字核对。 名字取《道德经》“天乃道，道乃久”。

开始提问

能做什么

  • 检索与细读原文：在历代中医古籍中检索原文，可限定时代，逐段细读上下文。
  • 考据与治学：方剂源流与加减化裁、药物性味归经的演变、术语的义项与盛衰、经文互见与异文、历代注家集注、训诂、句读、版本、避讳、度量衡。
  • 知识发现：发掘失传的关联、概念的演变、方剂的演化、隐含的关联与文献间的矛盾，提出研究假说，并用原文检验。
  • 证据核验：回答中的引文与原文逐字比对，标出一致、存疑与查无此段；也可以审计一个说法有没有文献依据。
  • 古今桥接：比较古代病证与现代医学在表型上的异同，不做回溯诊断。
  • 分析我的资料：上传自己的门诊处方、病案或调查表（CSV、Excel、Word 等），做描述统计、组间比较与处方用药分析，并与经典方比较。资料在本机读取并脱敏，不传到道乃久的服务器，相关对话不记录。

可以这样问

  • 桂枝汤的原方组成与剂量出自哪里？后世有哪些重要的加减化裁？
  • 「消渴」一词在历代文献中的含义与用法如何演变？请给出各时期的原文证据。
  • 比较历代注家对「太阳之为病，脉浮，头项强痛而恶寒」的解释，有哪些分歧？
  • 黄连的性味、归经与主治最早见于哪部书？后世有何变化？
  • 请审计这个说法：「六味地黄丸是张仲景在《伤寒论》中创制的」。

读哪些书

网站服务器载有全量语料：历代中医古籍九百余部，约 1.2 亿字，包括《黄帝内经》（素问、灵枢）、《难经》、《伤寒论》、 《金匮要略》、《神农本草经》、《备急千金要方》、《外台秘要》、《本草纲目》等。服务器暂不可用时，网页在浏览器内运行 按历代引用排序的要籍。各部古籍的来源、版本与授权，见语料来源与授权。

证据纪律

古籍说了什么，以检得的原文为准，不凭记忆补写；原文、注家与后世解释、计算推断、现代医学四个层次分开表述。 检索排序、关联规则与研究假说都是待检验的计算推断，须经专家核验。

使用须知

道乃久用于中医古籍的研究与学习，不提供诊断、处方或用药建议；历代度量衡的换算只是历史读法，不是剂量。 涉及身体健康的问题，请咨询合格的医生。

TaoChronos · Scientific Knowledge Discovery from Ancient TCM Texts

TaoChronos (道乃久) is a platform of AI agents for scientific knowledge discovery from the classical texts of Chinese medicine, developed by IMPF-AI (医哲未来人工智能研究院). It supports traceable research: it first retrieves the original text from the classics, then answers from it, and every quotation names its source and is checked against the original character by character. The name comes from the Daodejing: “天乃道，道乃久” (Heaven accords with the Dao; the Dao endures).

Start asking

What it can do

  • Search and close reading: search the classics of Chinese medicine across the dynasties, limit by period, and read each passage in context.
  • Textual scholarship: the origins and modifications of formulas, how herb properties and channel tropisms changed, the senses and fortunes of terms, parallel passages and variants, collected commentaries, glosses, punctuation, editions, taboo characters, and historical weights and measures.
  • Knowledge discovery: uncover lost associations, shifts in concepts, the evolution of formulas, hidden links and contradictions between texts; propose research hypotheses and test them against the original texts.
  • Evidence checking: quotations in answers are compared with the source character by character and marked as matching, in doubt or not found; you can also audit whether a claim has textual support.
  • Bridging past and present: compare the phenotypes of historical disorders with modern medicine, without retrospective diagnosis.
  • Analyse my data: upload your own clinic prescriptions, case records or survey forms (CSV, Excel, Word and more) for descriptive statistics, group comparisons and prescription analysis, compared with classical formulas. The data is read and de-identified on your device, never sent to TaoChronos's servers, and those conversations are not recorded.

Questions you can ask

  • Where do the original ingredients and doses of Guizhi Tang (桂枝汤) come from? What important modifications did later physicians make?
  • How did the meaning and use of the term 消渴 (xiaoke) change across the historical literature? Give textual evidence from each period.
  • Compare how commentators through the ages explained “太阳之为病，脉浮，头项强痛而恶寒”. Where do they disagree?
  • In which book do the properties, channel tropism and indications of Huanglian (黄连, coptis) first appear? How did they change later?
  • Please audit this claim: “Liuwei Dihuang Wan (六味地黄丸) was created by Zhang Zhongjing in the Shanghan Lun (伤寒论).”

Answers are in English; the classics are quoted in the original Chinese, each with an English translation.

Which books it reads

The site's server holds the full corpus: more than 900 classics of Chinese medicine, about 120 million characters, including the Huangdi Neijing (Suwen and Lingshu), Nanjing, Shanghan Lun, Jingui Yaolue, Shennong Bencao Jing, Beiji Qianjin Yaofang, Waitai Miyao and Bencao Gangmu. When the server is unavailable, the page runs the core classics, ranked by how often they were cited over the centuries, in your browser. For the source, edition and licence of each work, see Corpus sources and licences.

Evidence discipline

What the classics say is settled by the retrieved original text, never filled in from memory. The original text, commentators and later interpretation, computational inference, and modern medicine are kept as four separate layers. Search rankings, association rules and research hypotheses are computational inferences still to be tested, and must be checked by experts.

Terms of use

TaoChronos is for research on and study of the classical texts of Chinese medicine. It does not give diagnoses, prescriptions or medication advice; conversions of historical weights and measures are historical readings, not doses. For questions about your health, please consult a qualified doctor.
