# Legend of Fig. 1, the graphical abstract

The legend of the figure `graphical_abstract.py` draws, laid out like `paper/legends.md` (a title sentence, the parts
of the figure, then the statistics) and written in the manuscripts' own figure numbers. `build.py` sets it first among
the figure legends of the npj Digital Medicine manuscript; `engineering.md` carries a shorter caption of its own.

## Fig. 1 | Graphical abstract: a question and a dated corpus become findings that can be checked.

A scientist's question, fixed as a research contract before the first task, and the corpus are the two inputs.
Histogram, the 966 witnesses per 50 years by date of composition, a witness dated to a range spread evenly over it
(475 BCE–1949). Sixteen agents (a Director, centre, and fifteen specialists; filled, agents that may consult a language
model) work through 85 permissioned tools and may only propose. Commit hooks refuse a claim whose quotation is not
verbatim or does not state it, and an anachronism; validators grade the nine gates (G0–G8) and the research contract;
a human expert alone approves or rejects a hypothesis (gate G7, outlined). Every change is a typed event in an
append-only log, refused if its principal may not emit it, and each claim of a finding can be walked back to its
quotation, passage, witness, edition, date and licence. Bottom, what was measured, with rules alone and without
calling a language model: quotation checks, four probes on 60 random passages (light, verbatim quotations, accepted;
dark, corrupted quotations, refused); the 19 safety invariants (columns) on the intact agent (top row) and on six
variants with one safeguard removed each (vermillion, broken; 127 of 133 cells held); the 84 held-out hard
interpretive cases (vermillion, the two unsafe errors); and precision at 10 on the blind temporal benchmark of the
ranking computed from earlier texts (blue), of popularity (grey) and of chance (light grey; 20 random rankings) over
189 topic–cutoff pairs (lines, 95% confidence intervals). The agent, the corpus, one research run, verification and
research value are shown in full in Figs. 2–6.
