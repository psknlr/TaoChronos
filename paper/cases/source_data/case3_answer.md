##1. Meridian differentiation of lumbar pain — the classical basis

**Position of the disease:** the lumbar region is traversed by the Foot Taiyang Bladder channel and the Governor Vessel; the classical rule for lumbar pain is to take the popliteal region (distal):

> 「刺諸痛者，其脈皆實。故曰：從腰以上者，手太陰陽明皆主之，從腰以下者，足太陰陽明皆主之。病在上者，下取之。病在下者，高取之。病在頭者，取之足。病在腰者，取之膕。病生於頭者，頭重。生於手者，臂重。生於足者，足重。治病者，先刺其病所從生者也。」
> [[jc_a100.00065]] — *Huangdi Neijing · Lingshu*, conventionally pre-Qin–Western Han, ch. 9 *Zhongshi*

Zhang Jiebin's commentary (note: this is **a later commentator's explanation**, not the base text) glosses the popliteal rule as a *far-reaching* method:

> 「此遠取之法也，有病在上而脉通於下者，當取於下，病在下而脉通於上者，當取於上故在頭者取之足，在腰者取之膕，蓋疏其源而流自通，故諸經白有井榮俞原經合之辨。」
> [[jc_a402a.10486]] — *Leijing*, Ming dynasty, Zhang Jiebin

## 2. The point combination the tradition would build (teaching level — not yet his prescription)

**Layer A — local (near the site of pain):** 阿是穴 (pain-as-needle, 以痛为输) + 腰阳关 GV3 (Governor Vessel, L4 spinous process) + 肾俞 BL23 (L2,1.5 cun lateral, bilateral) + 大肠俞 BL25 (L4, 1.5 cun lateral, bilateral).
**Layer B — distal (per病在腰者取之膕):** 委中 BL40 (bilateral) + 昆仑 BL60.
**Layer C — upper–lower / extraordinary-vessel route:** 后溪 SI3 with 申脉 BL62 — the confluent pair through the Governor Vessel and Yang Qiao Mai, whose classical remit is 「目内眦、颈项、耳、肩、腰背」, i.e. the neck, shoulder and **low back**.

**Pairing logic that the records themselves generate:** 腰阳关·肾俞·大肠俞 (below, local) with 委中·昆仑 (below, distal channel) = *local–distal*; 后溪 (upper limb) with the lumbar points = *upper–lower*; bilateral 取穴 on the lumbar and popliteal points = *left–right*; Back-Shu points used locally = *Shu–local*. The Governor Vessel point and the Bladder points together cover the two channels that traverse the lumbar region.

**Draining vs. tonifying cannot be set yet — and that is exactly the point.** Once an examination establishes the pattern:
- 虚 → *"虚则补其母"*: Kidney belongs to Water; its mother is Metal, so the Kidney channel's Metal point (the Jing-River point, 大钟) is the mother point to tonify.
- 实 → *"实则泻其子"*: Water generates Wood, so the Kidney channel's Wood point (the Well point, 涌泉) is the child point to drain.
- 明言"不实不虚" → *"不实不虚，以经取之"*, i.e. take the same-phase point of the affected channel.
- The Five-Shu general rule that underpins the point classes: 「所出为井，所溜为荥，所注为腧，所行为经，所以为合二十七氣所行，皆在五腧也」[[cm_lingshu_sibucongkan.00093]] — *Huangdi Suwen Lingshu Jing (Zhaofu Jujing tang ben)*, pre-Qin–Western Han, juan 1; and the indications: 「井主心下滿，荥主身熱，俞主體重節痛，經主喘咳寒热，合主逆气而泄」[[jc_a300.00107]] — *Bashi Yi Nanjing*, Eastern Han, juan 68 (俞穴 "body heaviness and joint pain" is the classic line for the heavy, aching lumbar patient).
- Guardrail worth stating: an "Earth" point is not Spleen tonification, and "kidney deficiency" cannot be manufactured from the name of a point; the platform's own audit explicitly blocks that move.

**Caution recorded for 委中:** 「委中(一名血　)　在　中央約紋動脈陷中伏臥取之膀胱脈所入為合。(針五分。禁灸)治熱病汗不出。」[[jc_k008.00953]] — *Zhenjiu Fengyuan*, Qing, Li Xuechuan, juan 4 — needling close to the artery, and *moxibustion prohibited* in this record.

## 4. Safety — this part is not optional

- **Eight months of recurrent low back pain should be medically assessed before any needling.** Nothing in the case record excludes disc herniation with radiculopathy, spinal stenosis, inflammatory disease, or malignancy. Please seek medical evaluation urgently if there is: pain radiating below the knee, numbness or weakness in the leg, saddle/perineal numbness, bladder or bowel dysfunction, fever, unexplained weight loss, a history of cancer, steroid use, or trauma. Acupuncture is not a substitute for imaging and orthopaedic/pain assessment.
- The review's triage entries for acute neurological signs and for aetiological work-up came back **undetermined**, precisely because the relevant observations are unknown rather than negative — unknown was not treated as "no".
- Needling must be performed by a qualified practitioner after examination, with sterile single-use needles; for this patient, perineal/lower-lumbar and popliteal needling require the usual anatomical caution, and 委中 is a point next to an artery (and recorded as 禁灸 in one source).
- Note the layer separation the platform keeps: the classical rationale above is a **statement of why the tradition chooses these points**, not evidence of benefit for this man. The only modern evidence attached is for acupuncture *in general* — not these points, not this method, not this case.
