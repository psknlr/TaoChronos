## 3. The formula: source line, composition, and the doses as written

**Textual lineage (corpus evidence).** The 15‑drug formula descends from a Tang formulation recorded under a different name: 「《古今錄驗》療腰痛，皆猶腎氣虛弱，臥冷濕地，當風所得，不時差，久久流入腳膝，冷痺疼弱重滯，或偏枯，腰腳疼攣，腳重急痛，獨活續斷湯方。」 with the note 「《肘後》有附子，無續斷、甘草、牛膝、人參、當歸，止十二味。」 [[jc_d004a.01483]] (唐·王焘《外台秘要》卷十七). The earliest witness in this corpus under the *name* 独活寄生汤 is 南宋·陈言《三因极一病证方论》(1174): 「獨活寄生湯　治厯節風近人用之甚效亦治腰背痛」 [[sanyin_fang.003.00216]]. Later: 《妇人大全良方》(1237) under 婦人腰痛方論 [[jc_g005.00820]], 《仁斋直指》(1264) under 腰痛證治 [[renzhai_zhizhi.018.01971]], 《世医得效方》(1328–45) under 脚氣 [[shiyi_dexiao.009.01625]], and 《医方集解》(1682), whose header records 「提要：風寒溼痹　出處：千金」 [[jc_d075a.00357]] — note that the 千金 attribution is 汪昂's; my search of this corpus did **not** return a 《千金方》 witness for this composition.

**Composition as written** (元·《世医得效方》): 「獨活（三兩）　桑寄生（無真者續斷代）　杜仲（製炒去絲）　細辛（去苗）　牛膝（去苗酒浸）　秦艽（去心）　茯苓（去皮）　白芍藥　桂心（不見火）　芎藭　防風（去蘆）　甘草（炙）　人參　熟地黄（洗蒸）　當歸（去蘆各二）」 [[shiyi_dexiao.009.01625]]; the women's variant in 《妇人大全良方》 is 「獨活（三兩）　桑寄生　續斷　杜仲　北細辛　川牛膝　秦艽　茯苓　白芍藥　桂心　川芎防風　人參　熟地黃　當歸（各二兩）」 [[jc_g005.00820]]. 《三因》 writes all fifteen at equal dose (各三兩); 《仁斋直指》 has 独活二兩半 and closes with 「下利者去地黄」 [[renzhai_zhizhi.018.01971]]; 《医方集解》 writes 「等分，每服四錢」 [[jc_d075a.00357]].

*Metrological note:* the 两 here are Song–Yuan-period measures; the tool's scholarly conversion estimates (e.g. 独活三两 ≈ 111.9–123.9 g in the Song–Yuan system) are **readings of the historical text, not a modern dose** [[shiyi_dexiao.009.01625]]. Any present-day dosing must be set by the prescribing practitioner.

## 4. Rationale of the composition and the herb combinations

The clearest statement of the design logic is 汪昂's 方义 in 《医方集解》:

「此足少陰厥陰藥也， 獨活細辛入少陰，通血脈，偕秦艽防風疏經升陽以祛風，桑寄生益氣血，祛風濕， 偕杜仲牛膝健骨強筋而固下，芎歸芍地所以活血而補陰，參桂苓草所以益氣而補陽， 辛溫以散之，甘溫以補之，使血氣足而風溼除，則肝腎強而痹痛愈矣。」 [[jc_d075a.00357]]

Decomposed into five functional clusters:

1. **祛风湿、通经络、止痹痛（治标）**: 独活 + 细辛 + 秦艽 + 防风. 独活's own 药性 in the 本草 tradition: 「味苦，平。主风寒所击」 [[jc_c000.00153]] (《神农本草经》), and 《名医别录》 gives it 甘、微温; 汪昂 pairs 独活、细辛 with the kidney channel, while 秦艽、防风 疏经升阳以祛风.
2. **温经散寒（针对"遇寒加重"）**: 桂心. 汪昂 quotes 朱丹溪 on this point for chronic low‑back pain: 「{朱丹溪曰：久腰痛必用官桂以開之方止，腹脅痛亦然。}」 [[jc_d075a.00357]]
3. **补肝肾、强筋骨、固下（治本 — the key for 腰膝酸软无力）**: 桑寄生 + 杜仲 + 牛膝. 杜仲's classical indication is exactly the patient's: 「味辛甘，平，溫，無毒。主腰脊痛，補中，益精氣，堅筋骨，強志」 [[jc_d002.00526]] (唐·孙思邈《千金翼方》卷三). 桑寄生 「味甘苦。入足少阴经。」 [[jc_c032.00272]] (清《本草撮要》); 牛膝 「味苦，酸」 [[jc_c000.00127]] (《神农本草经》), and in this formula it also 引血下行, so the 桂、细辛、牛膝 trio guides cold down through the channels.
4. **养血活血、荣筋止痛（治风先治血）**: 当归 + 川芎 + 芍药 + 熟地黄 — 汪昂: 「芎歸芍地所以活血而補陰」, so that wind‑damp has a nourished sinew to be expelled from.
5. **益气健脾（扶正以祛邪）**: 人参 + 茯苓 + 甘草 — 汪昂: 「參桂苓草所以益氣而補陽」, which is what makes the formula usable in a **deficient** rather than merely excess patient, and why 朱丹溪 said 久腰痛必用官桂.

**Structural point:** the formula is deliberately half expelling, half supporting — exactly what 虚实夹杂 (deficiency plus cold‑damp) demands, and the reason it is not interchangeable with 蠲痹汤 (pure expelling) or 甘姜苓术汤 (pure warming).

## 7. Safety notes on this formula

- **细辛** — aristolochic acid concerns relate to the aerial parts; volatile‑oil toxicity; the classical rule 「细辛不过钱」 is itself a limit‑dose reminder.
- **甘草** — glycyrrhizin: pseudoaldosteronism and hypokalaemia with long or high use (relevant to blood pressure and potassium).
- **马兜铃 is *not* an ingredient** of this formula; it appeared in the safety scan only because I named it in the query text. The genuine watch‑items here are 细辛 and 甘草.
- **附子/川乌** appear only in the 《肘后》 and 《宣明论方》 variants (cold‑severe yang‑deficiency); if that branch is chosen, aconite toxicity requires professional handling.
- **Quality note with clinical meaning:** 桑寄生 is expensive and often substituted — the original texts already allow 续断 as substitute («無真者續斷代»), so the source and authenticity of 桑寄生/独活 matter.

## 8. What remains uncertain and should be verified by a human

1. **Tongue and pulse are missing** from the presentation. They can overturn the pattern: red tongue with yellow‑greasy coat or hot, swollen, night‑worse joints ⇒ 湿热痹 (this formula would be wrong); thin red coat with a floating‑rapid pulse ⇒ yin deficiency with heat; fixed stabbing pain with a purple tongue ⇒ blood stasis (a different method entirely). The classical cases themselves turned on the pulse (弦𦂳, 浮數按之遲緩, 沉細).
2. **Whether 腰膝酸软无力 is truly liver–kidney deficiency** or simply disuse/deconditioning of a chronic painful joint — this distinction is what separates 独活寄生汤 from 蠲痹汤.
3. **Whether there is effusion, morning stiffness, or joint deformity** — needed both for the TCM distinction (着痹/骨痹/鹤膝风) and for the modern work‑up.
4. **Modern assessment is required in parallel:** at 58 with 3‑year bilateral knee pain, imaging, inflammatory markers, and bone‑density/osteoporosis assessment (post‑menopausal) belong with, not after, the herbal decision.
5. **Provenance points to check by hand:** the 《医方集解》 attribution 「出處：千金」 was not confirmed against a 《千金方》 witness in this corpus; and the corpus's earliest named witness (《三因》, 1174) post‑dates the Tang 独活续断汤 (752) that carries the same 15 drugs.

### One‑line summary

Cold‑aggravated, 3‑year bilateral knee pain with lumbar and knee soreness and weakness in a 58‑year‑old woman is, in classical terms, **痹证·久痹 with 肝肾亏虚 and 寒湿痹阻 (肾经腰痛)**; the literature's standing answer is **独活寄生汤** — expelling wind‑cold‑damp (独活、秦艽、防风、细辛、桂) *while* nourishing liver and kidney and blood (桑寄生、杜仲、牛膝、当归、川芎、芍药、熟地、人参、茯苓、甘草), with the historically documented add‑ons (虎骨/骨碎补 for bone‑level disease, 黄芪/续断 for marked qi‑blood deficiency, 陈皮 for wind without heat) and the documented exclusions (地黄 if loose stools; and the whole formula if the picture is 湿热).
