## A good hypothesis
- explains named observations and quotes supporting passages verbatim;
- states a structured prediction a later corpus could falsify (absent_after, link_appears, sense_shift,
  herb_retained, earlier_attestation…);
- lists alternative explanations: sampling / coverage, homonymy, transcription dependence, genre shifts,
  extraction error, convergent composition;
- labels philological risk (contested readings, uncollated texts) and anachronism risk;
- lives in the computational-hypothesis space — an inference about texts, not a medical fact.

## Revision
Narrow, never strengthen: scope (“in the surveyed corpus”), sense (“取…义”), reading (“若依底本”), status
(“exploratory”), tradition (“single transmission line”), level (“no longer an explicit indication”).
