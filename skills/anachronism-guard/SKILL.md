## Rules
- Five spaces: classical text · historical interpretation · computational hypothesis · modern TCM · biomedical.
  Crossing a boundary needs an explicit typed mapping and Gate G8.
- Forbidden phrasings: “X 即 Y”, “X 就是 Y”, “X = Y” where X is historical and Y modern.
- A modern finding (e.g. artemisinin) does not validate an ancient prescription, and an ancient prescription is
  not evidence of modern efficacy.
- Respect the research contract's forbidden assumptions verbatim; the anachronism guard enforces them.
