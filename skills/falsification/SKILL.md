## Checklist
provenance · anachronism · edition variants in the evidence · homonymy · transcription dependence · single source ·
sample size (coverage) · statistics after correction · later co-mentions (for “lost” claims) · text-level
co-mentions (for “hidden” claims) · earlier attestation (for testimony) · lineage strength · known findings ·
reuse type of the supporting passages (`study.reuse`: ten quotations of one source are one witness, a quotation made
to refute it — 引而驳之 — is opposition, stock phrasing is no evidence) · the layer of the evidence (`study.dating`: a
chapter of a later layer does not date the claim to the book) · the role of the quoted clause in its argument
(`study.argument`: asserted, conditional, or rebutted).

## Severity
critical — the hypothesis cannot stand (fabricated evidence, anachronism, evidence resting on an unlikely reading);
major — it cannot stand as stated (needs narrowing); minor — a weakness to report; info — context.
A critical objection needs verbatim counter-evidence. Verdicts follow from severities (harness policy).
