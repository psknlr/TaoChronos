## Method
1. **Name first.** Collect every name of the target — 本名, 异名, 避讳改名 (薯蓣→薯药→山药, 玄参→元参, 丸→圆) — and
   read a formula's name also where a book prints it only as the heading of the entry (笈成: 地黄丸 / 治…… / 熟地黄八钱……).
2. **Date by the latest possible date.** "Earliest" means the smallest terminus ante quem: a book dated only by its
   dynasty does not precede one dated to a reign. Commentary layers carry the date of the commentator.
3. **Formulas.** Group written-out compositions by their drugs (drug families: 桂枝/桂心/肉桂). 原方 = the earliest
   tradition under the name asked for; 通行方 = the most-witnessed one; 加减化裁 and 同名异方 are kept apart; ratios compare
   witnesses independently of the period's measures; doses are read in the measures of their own period and are never
   dosage guidance.
4. **Drugs.** One entry per work (the fullest copy); record the first statement of each property — 归经 and 升降浮沉 are
   Jin–Yuan doctrines, their absence from the 本经 is expected, not a gap.
5. **Terms.** Compare shares of passages per period (Wilson intervals), not raw counts; test the trend; read the first
   attestations and the densest works; senses are period-bound and modern concepts are candidates, never equivalents.
   The sense history (`study.senses`) dates where the shares of the curated senses shift and gathers the uses no
   curated sense fits into candidate senses — for a person to read and name.
6. **Recurrence.** Concordance separates other copies of the same work (同书异本), explicit quotations (引文) and
   restatements (互见); the apparatus lists 异文/脱/衍/倒 by witness.

## Pitfalls
- A copy is not an independent witness; a quotation dates the quoted text no later than the quoting book.
- Taboo evidence (避讳) dates an edition, not the composition of the work; it is indicative, not proof.
- Rule-based extraction is machine reading: quote the witness and let the reader check it.
